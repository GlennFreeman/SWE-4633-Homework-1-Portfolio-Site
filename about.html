What about Glenn?

Its always about you, never how are you 😭

Hi I'm Glenn Freeman. I am a non-traditional student and this is my second attempt at college 😱

Origins

I am originally from Griffin and went to Clayton State University for my first time around at college. Now I currently reside in Douglasville and commute around an hour to class 🙃

College

I am a Software Engineering major. The reason I like taking classes in person is that I have ADHD and that makes online classes more difficult for me to grasp the material as I usually need guidance.

Future

Career wise I would love to become a Software Developer. I am not too sure of what particular niche I fit into yet, but the act of coding brings me joy, even in this AI heavy future.

Hobbies

Hobbies I enjoy are: gaming (Tabletop and Electronic), 3D printing, Art (generative art and the likes using programing and GIMP), and finally I dabble in indie game development.

Mental Health

One other thing I would like everyone who gets this far to know is, your mental health is important. The reason I am doing a second attempt at college is that my first time I had to drop out due to the undiagnosed ADHD. It caused me much anxiety, that when untreated turned into agoraphobia and depression. It wasn't a great time. The good news is, we have resources available to us as students to address these types of issues, for FREE! I highly recommend taking some time to just sit with yourself and ask if you feel optimal when it comes to your emotions. Yes school is stressful but if you start to get overwhelmed or burnt out, I will make a strong bet that seeing CAPS can help you.

I could go on and on about taking care of your mental health, but suffice to say, "ain't nobody reading all that." I just wish you all the best, you got this!
